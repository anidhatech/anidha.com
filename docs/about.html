A young company with experienced engineers

Anidha Tech Solutions LLP was incorporated in Hyderabad in February 2026. The company is new; the people leading it are not.

Who we are

Our leadership brings more than 19 years of industry experience in infrastructure and platform engineering.

We started Anidha to give enterprises and growing businesses a different kind of engineering partner: small enough that senior engineers do the work, and rigorous enough to be trusted with systems that matter.

We are technology-agnostic. We build software, platforms, cloud infrastructure and AI systems in whatever stack fits the problem and the team that will own it.
