How the practices fit together

Real systems cross boundaries. An industrial monitoring platform, for example, needs device ingestion and time-series data (industrial IoT), a multi-tenant web application (software engineering), a clear interface for plant teams (design), infrastructure that scales (cloud and DevOps) and ongoing support (managed services).

We put one lead in charge of the whole outcome and bring in specialists as each stage needs them. You deal with one team, one plan and one point of accountability.
