How we work

Five stages, each ending in something you can review. You always know what was decided, what was built and what comes next.

  1. Discover

    Understand the goal, the current systems and the constraints.

    You get an assessment and a roadmap.

  2. Design

    Agree the architecture, security model and delivery plan.

    You get architecture decisions and estimates.

  3. Build

    Ship in short iterations, with tests and infrastructure as code.

    You get working software every sprint.

  4. Launch

    Release safely, with load testing, hardening and rollback ready.

    You get a production release and runbooks.

  5. Evolve

    Measure, support and improve, or hand over to your team.

    You get regular reviews and knowledge transfer.

Ways to work with us

Pick the model that matches how much you want to own. You can start with a fixed-scope piece and continue as a team.

Fixed scope

A defined outcome, such as an MVP, a migration or an assessment, with an agreed plan and milestones.

Best when the problem is clear and you want a predictable delivery.

Team extension

Senior engineers join your team, work in your tools and follow your rituals, with our leadership behind them.

Best when you own the roadmap and need more experienced hands.

Managed service

We run and improve a system for you: monitoring, incidents, patching, cost and change.

Best when a system matters but should not consume your team.
