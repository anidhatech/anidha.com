Privacy notice

How we handle personal data when you visit www.anidha.com or contact us, and how to use your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Last updated 15 November 2026

In short

  • This website has no forms, no accounts, no advertising and no cookies. You can read every page without telling us who you are.
  • We receive personal data only when you choose to email or call us, and we use it only to reply and to discuss the work you asked about.
  • Our hosting provider counts visits anonymously, without cookies, so we can see how the site is used and how fast it loads.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing contact@anidha.com.

Who we are

Anidha Tech Solutions LLP, Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("Anidha", "we", "us") decides why and how the personal data described here is processed. Under the DPDP Act, that makes us the Data Fiduciary for it.

What personal data we process, and why

WhenPersonal dataPurpose
You email or call usYour name, email address or phone number, organisation and job title if you give them, and what you write or tell usTo reply, understand your needs and discuss or deliver the work you asked about

You provide email, phone and job-application data voluntarily, for the purpose you contact us about. We process it for that purpose only, as the DPDP Act permits for data that is voluntarily provided (section 7(a)). We do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to keep you informed, and you can withdraw that at any time.

Please do not send us passwords, keys, health information or other sensitive personal data. If a project needs any, we will agree a secure way to share it.

Cookies, storage and analytics

  • No cookies. The website does not set cookies.
  • Visit analytics. The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which runs Cloudflare Web Analytics on it. Cloudflare states that this works without cookies or local storage and does not track visitors across websites. We see only totals, such as page views, referring sites, countries and page-load times, never individual visitors.
  • Seasonal greeting. During festive periods, the site may keep one setting in your browser's session storage so that a greeting is shown only once per visit. It contains no personal data and is deleted when you close the tab.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers, who act as our Data Processors and process data only on our instructions:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the website and provides the visit analytics described above.
  • Our email and telephone providers carry the messages and calls you send us.

Some of these providers process data outside India. The DPDP Act allows this except to countries the Government of India restricts. We will stop using a provider if its country is restricted.

We may disclose personal data where Indian law requires it, for example to comply with a lawful request from a government authority.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and correspondence: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start working together, in which case we keep it for the length of the engagement and as long as contract, tax and accounting law requires afterwards.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months after the role is filled, unless you ask us to keep your details for future roles, or we hire you.
  • Website technical data: held by our hosting providers under their own retention periods; we keep only aggregated statistics.

When personal data is no longer needed, or when you withdraw your consent, we delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.

How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the website, access to mailboxes restricted to the people who need it, multi-factor authentication on our accounts, and deleting data we no longer need. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected, as the DPDP Act requires.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your personal data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent to any processing that relies on it, as easily as you gave it. This does not affect processing already done.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance about how we handle your personal data.

How to make a request or raise a grievance

Email contact@anidha.com with the subject line "Privacy request", or call +91 77998 32255 (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST). Requests are handled by the partner responsible for data protection at Anidha.

We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We aim to respond within 30 days, and we resolve grievances within 90 days at most. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and professionals and is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf. If we learn that we hold a child's personal data without a parent's or guardian's verifiable consent, we will delete it.

Our products, such as TechElevate and JEE Command Centre, have their own privacy notices that describe the data they process. JEE Command Centre asks for a parent's or guardian's approval before syncing data for users under 18.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, including our products and our social media pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Changes to this notice
