Context

Machine condition monitoring is one of MVS ACMEI's businesses. Its vibration sensors attach to rotating equipment such as pumps, fans, motors and gearboxes, in plants where one unplanned stoppage can halt a production line.

Sensors alone do not prevent failures. MVS ACMEI needed a platform that syncs readings from every device reliably and turns them into a clear picture of machine health, for every customer and plant it serves, running on cloud infrastructure that can grow with the fleet.

Problem

Three groups needed different things from the same data:

  • Plant teams needed to see, at a glance, which machines were healthy and which needed attention.
  • Analysts needed vibration spectra and history to diagnose faults such as bearing wear or looseness.
  • MVS ACMEI's administrators needed to onboard customers and plants, move sensors between machines, manage users and update firmware without risk.

All of it had to work across many customers, with each customer seeing only their own plants.

Solution

We moved the platform onto AWS, with infrastructure defined as code, and enhanced it across the stack: device data sync, time-series storage, the web application and administration.

The application is organised the way plants are: customer, plant, area, equipment, sensor location and sensor. Every level rolls up a condition, so a problem on one sensor is visible from the top.

Shop-floor views

Every machine in a plant on one board, with horizontal, vertical and axial velocity, temperature and signal strength per sensor location, coloured by ISO 10816 vibration limits.

FFT spectral analysis

On-demand and scheduled captures with waveform, spectrum, peak table and waterfall history, so developing faults are visible long before they are audible.

Live trends and alerts

Telemetry every ten seconds, trend bands, and zone-breach alerts with hysteresis so that brief spikes do not flood teams with notifications. A per-device signal log shows exactly what each sensor did and when.

Roles that fit the plant

Administrators manage customers, plants, sensors and people; analysts tune limits and machine configuration; plant users see only the plants assigned to them.

Safe fleet administration

Sensor assignment and moves, firmware version tracking, and over-the-air firmware updates behind an explicit unlock step.

Accountability

An activity log records who changed what, the result, and any refused or failed attempts, alongside alert-rule templates for consistent configuration.
