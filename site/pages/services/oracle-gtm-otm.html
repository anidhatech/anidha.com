Oracle Global Trade Management

Cross-border trade compliance, classification and customs, part of Oracle Fusion Cloud Supply Chain & Manufacturing.

  • Trade compliance and screening. Restricted-party, embargo and sanctions screening of parties, orders and shipments, with review workflows so compliance teams resolve matches quickly.
  • Product classification. Tariff, Harmonized System (HS), ECCN and Schedule B classification across the countries you trade with, including bulk classification and content loads.
  • Licences and controls. Export licence and import permit management, with checks that stop a shipment when a licence is missing or exhausted.
  • Customs management. Electronic customs filings and trade documents such as commercial invoices, packing lists and certificates of origin.
  • Landed cost. Estimated landed cost, covering transport, handling, insurance, duties and taxes, to compare sourcing and routing options before you commit.
  • Trade programmes. Duty drawback, bonded warehouse and free trade zone programmes, set up where they apply to your business.

Oracle Transportation Management

Planning, executing and paying for freight across modes, carriers and regions.

  • Orders and shipments. Order release, shipment building and consolidation across inbound, outbound and multi-leg moves.
  • Planning and optimisation. Multi-stop, multi-mode planning with the business rules, constraints and costs that reflect how you actually ship.
  • Rates and carrier tendering. Rate set-up and maintenance, carrier selection and tendering, with carrier collaboration for acceptance and status.
  • Visibility and events. Shipment tracking and event management, so exceptions surface before a customer asks.
  • Freight settlement. Freight audit, invoice matching, settlement and payment, with accruals your finance team can trust.
  • GTM and OTM together. One shipment record from planning to customs: screening, classification and trade documents flow from the same data as transport.

How an implementation runs

Five stages, each ending in something you can review and sign off.

  1. Assess

    Map your trade lanes, countries, compliance obligations and transport flows.

    You get a scope and a phased plan.

  2. Design

    Agree screening rules, classification approach, planning rules and integration design.

    You get a signed-off solution design.

  3. Configure

    Configure GTM and OTM, load master and trade content, and build integrations.

    You get a working system in a test environment.

  4. Test

    Run user acceptance tests with real trade and shipping scenarios, and automate regression.

    You get test evidence and a go-live checklist.

  5. Go live

    Cut over, run hypercare, then move to steady-state support.

    You get a live system and runbooks.
