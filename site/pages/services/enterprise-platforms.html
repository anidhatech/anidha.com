What we do

Capability. This practice draws on our team's experience from previous roles. We have not yet published an Anidha case study for it, and we are happy to discuss relevant experience in detail.

  • Implementation and rollout. Configuring the platform around your processes, with data migration and cut-over planning.
  • Integration. Connecting SAP or Salesforce to the rest of your estate through APIs, middleware and events.
  • Customisation. Extensions and custom objects that stay upgrade-safe.
  • Support and enhancement. Ongoing fixes, small changes and release management after go-live.

How we approach it

  • Start from your process, not the platform default.
  • Keep customisation minimal and documented, so upgrades stay cheap.
  • Treat integration as software: versioned, tested and monitored.
