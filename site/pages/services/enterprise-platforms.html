Oracle Global Trade Management

Capability

Oracle Global Trade Management (GTM) is Oracle's cloud application for cross-border trade compliance, part of Oracle Fusion Cloud Supply Chain & Manufacturing and often run alongside Oracle Transportation Management (OTM). We implement, configure, integrate and support it.

  • Trade compliance and screening. Restricted-party, embargo and sanctions screening of parties, orders and shipments, with review workflows for matches.
  • Product classification. Tariff, Harmonized System (HS), ECCN and Schedule B classification across the countries you trade with.
  • Customs management. Electronic customs filings, trade documents such as commercial invoices and certificates of origin, and import and export licence control.
  • Landed cost. Estimated landed cost, covering transport, handling, insurance, duties and taxes, to compare sourcing and routing options.
  • Trade programmes. Setting up duty drawback, bonded warehouse and free trade zone programmes where they apply.
  • Integration. Connecting GTM to OTM, Oracle Fusion and other ERPs, so orders, shipments and screening results flow without re-keying.
