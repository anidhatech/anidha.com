What we do

  • Cloud foundations. Accounts, networking, identity and guardrails set up as code, ready for teams to build on.
  • Kubernetes and platforms. Clusters and internal developer platforms that give teams self-service with safe defaults.
  • CI/CD and DevSecOps. Pipelines with automated testing, security scanning and progressive delivery.
  • Migration. Moving workloads to the cloud, or between clouds, in planned and reversible stages.
  • FinOps. Making cloud spend visible, attributable and controlled, without slowing teams down.

How we approach it

  • Everything is infrastructure as code, reviewed like any other change.
  • Least-privilege access and audit logging from the start.
  • Costs are tagged and reported so you can see what each team and workload spends.
