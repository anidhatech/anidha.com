Discuss your project

What we do

  • Research. Interviews, task analysis and usability testing with the people who will use the product.
  • Interaction and visual design. Flows, wireframes and high-fidelity screens grounded in that research.
  • Design systems. Reusable components and tokens that keep a product consistent as it grows.
  • Accessibility. Designs and builds checked against WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard and screen-reader use.

How we approach it

  • Designers and engineers work in the same team, so designs are buildable.
  • Prototypes are tested with real users before they are built.
  • Accessibility is a requirement from the first sketch, not an audit at the end.

Typical stack

User research, prototyping, design systems, WCAG 2.2, usability testing.
