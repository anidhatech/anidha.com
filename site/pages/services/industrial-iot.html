From sensor signal to a decision on the shop floor

Sensor-to-dashboard systems for condition monitoring, real-time analytics and predictive maintenance in heavy industry.

Discuss your project

What we do

  • Device ingestion. Receiving telemetry and waveform uploads from sensors reliably, including over poor connections.
  • Time-series analytics. Storing and querying high-frequency readings, with trends and history that stay fast.
  • Spectral analysis. FFT spectra, peak tables and waterfall history for vibration analysis.
  • Alerting. Zone-based alerts with hysteresis, so plant teams are told about real changes and not noise.
  • Multi-tenant dashboards. Customer, plant, area and equipment hierarchies with role-based access.

How we approach it

  • Model the plant the way operators think about it.
  • Design for intermittent connectivity and noisy data.
  • Deliver in phases, so the platform is useful early and grows with confidence.

Typical stack

Device APIs, PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB, WebSockets, FFT processing, React dashboards, AWS.

We work with what you already run where it makes sense.
