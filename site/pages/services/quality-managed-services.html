What we do

Capability. This practice draws on our team's experience from previous roles. We have not yet published an Anidha case study for it, and we are happy to discuss relevant experience in detail.

  • Test automation. Unit, API and end-to-end suites that run in your pipeline on every change.
  • Performance testing. Load and soak tests against realistic traffic before it reaches production.
  • Site reliability engineering. Service levels, observability and incident practice that reduce surprises.
  • Run and support. Monitoring, patching, incident response and small enhancements under an agreed scope, in Indian business hours (see coverage below).

How we approach it

  • Agree what "working" means as measurable service levels.
  • Automate the checks people would otherwise forget.
  • Review incidents without blame and fix the cause, not only the symptom.

Coverage and response times

Managed services are delivered in Indian business hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST (UTC+5:30), excluding public holidays. That covers most of the Gulf working day (7:30 to 16:30 GST). For clients in the US it falls outside your working hours, so we agree handover times and written status updates up front.

For each engagement we agree in writing what is covered, how incidents are prioritised and the response time for each priority within those hours. We report against those targets every month.

We do not currently offer 24×7 on-call cover. If your system needs it, we will tell you at the start and plan the out-of-hours arrangement with your team, for example automated alerting and runbooks your own on-call staff can follow.
